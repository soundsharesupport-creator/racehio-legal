1. In short

  • RACEHIO has no accounts and no RACEHIO servers. Races and sharing happen directly between nearby devices.
  • RACEHIO contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services from other companies.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not track you across apps or websites.
  • Most information stays on your device. It leaves your device only when you race with people nearby, share a car, save or share a picture or a video, or write to support. While RACEHIO is open, your nickname and avatar emoji and colour are also visible to nearby devices (see section 5).

2. Information stored on your device

RACEHIO stores the following in its own storage on your device:

  • your display name and avatar (an emoji and a colour, or a picture you chose); the display name is a nickname you choose and does not need to be your real name; saved avatar pictures are also kept on this device;
  • the cars you make (body shape, paint and patterns, parts, wheels and name) and unsaved drafts;
  • cars you received from other people, with a note of the car they were first copied from (see section 6);
  • race history and results (up to 200 races), photo-finish pictures and replays of your most recent races (up to 30);
  • settings such as appearance, language, sound, haptics and invitations;
  • the players you chose to hide (their nickname and the random identifier of their RACEHIO install), so you no longer get their race invitations or cars; you can show them again in Settings › Hidden Players;
  • whether RACEHIO Garage was last confirmed as active, so the app can show your plan before StoreKit answers;
  • which car is your free car on RACEHIO Free (see section 8);
  • a random install identifier kept in this device’s Keychain (see section 5).

3. Motion sensors

During a race you steer by tilting the phone, and before the start you can look around by turning it. For this RACEHIO reads the device’s motion sensors only while the race screen is open. The readings are used on the device straight away; they are not stored and do not leave the device. iOS does not ask for a permission for this.

4. Photos and videos

To use a picture as your avatar, you pick it with the system photo picker. RACEHIO receives only the picture you choose. It keeps a local gallery of cropped 512 × 512 pixel copies of each picture you use as an avatar, so you can pick it again later; you can delete these copies in the avatar editor. RACEHIO does not use the camera.

A photo-finish picture or a replay video is saved to your Photos library only when you ask for it (Save or Save Video). For this, RACEHIO asks for permission to add to Photos; it never reads your library. The video is made on your device from the replay of that race. Share on a replay gives the same video as Save: it is made on your device and kept on this device, so you can save or share it again without waiting; RACEHIO deletes older videos by itself, and iOS may remove them when storage runs low. When you tap Share, the picture or the video is passed to the iOS share sheet and goes only where you choose to send it (for example Instagram, TikTok or Messages); RACEHIO does not upload it anywhere itself.

5. Nearby races and sharing (Local Network)

RACEHIO connects nearby devices directly to each other with Apple’s Bonjour and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi to find nearby players, send invitations, run races and share cars. The devices do not need to be on the same Wi-Fi network; if they are, RACEHIO may also connect through it. Local Network permission is required for this.

While RACEHIO is open on screen, it shows your nickname and your avatar’s emoji and colour (never an avatar picture) to nearby RACEHIO devices so friends can send you a car or invite you to a race. This happens even when race invitations are turned off in Settings. It stops when you close RACEHIO or it goes to the background; if race invitations are on, iOS may keep RACEHIO reachable for invitations for a short time after you leave the app. When you host a race, the race name and your nickname are also shown to nearby devices so others can find and join it.

Each name shown to nearby devices begins with a short part of a random install identifier. RACEHIO creates this identifier once and keeps it in this device’s Keychain, so it stays the same even if you delete and reinstall the app. It lets races and sharing reach the right device. It is random, contains no personal data and is not linked to any account.

During a nearby race, RACEHIO exchanges with the other participants’ devices:

  • your nickname and avatar (emoji and colour, and a small copy of your avatar picture if you set one);
  • the car you race with;
  • your driving during the race (gas, brake and steering, sent as numbers; never the sensor readings themselves) and your car’s position;
  • the race name, lobby settings (including the track and the stadium appearance chosen by the host), readiness status, race start, progress, results and leaving the race;
  • random identifiers that keep the race in sync (a random player identifier created by RACEHIO when you first set up your profile; it stays the same between races and is not linked to your identity).

This data goes directly between the devices; it does not pass through a RACEHIO server. RACEHIO does not add its own encryption to race data, so do not put private information in your nickname or race names. Other participants’ devices keep the results of races you ran together, including your nickname and car, in their own race history and replays.

6. Sharing cars

When you share a car through AirDrop, Messages, Files or another app, the system sends the car file you chose. A car file (.racehio) contains the car, its name, when it was created and last changed, a random identifier of the car it was first copied from, and the RACEHIO version that made it.

With nearby sharing, RACEHIO first sends the nearby device you picked a request with your nickname, your avatar’s emoji and colour, and the car’s name and a small preview picture. The request is not encrypted by RACEHIO. The car file itself is sent only after the owner of that device accepts, and the file is encrypted with keys created for that one exchange.

7. Notifications

If you allow notifications and someone nearby invites you to a race just after you leave RACEHIO, you get a local notification. This works only for a short time (usually up to about half a minute) while iOS still keeps RACEHIO running in the background. After that iOS pauses the app, and invitations reach you only when you open RACEHIO again. There is no push-notification server.

8. RACEHIO Free and your free car

On RACEHIO Free you have one car of your own that you can change at any time. To know which car that is, RACEHIO stores the identifier of that car in the app’s storage and in this device’s Keychain. It is not synced through iCloud, is not used for tracking and is not sent anywhere. Cars you received from other people are never counted as your free car.

9. RACEHIO Garage

Purchases, renewals, cancellations, refunds and Restore Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive your payment details. RACEHIO checks on your device whether your Apple Account has an active RACEHIO Garage subscription.

10. Contacting support

If you write to support from the app, your message is sent from your own email app. It includes the subject and text you wrote, and the RACEHIO version, iOS version and device type to help us answer. We use this only to reply to you.

11. Keeping and deleting information

You can delete cars and race history in the app, remove saved avatar pictures in the avatar editor, and change your nickname and avatar at any time. Older races and replays are removed automatically beyond the limits in section 2. Deleting RACEHIO removes its data from your device; the free car record and the install identifier in the Keychain may remain, even after you reinstall the app, until the device is erased. Copies of race results, replays and shared cars kept by other people are on their devices and are not controlled by us.

12. Children

RACEHIO is intended for people who meet the age rating shown for RACEHIO on the App Store and the minimum age required by the laws of their country.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, EEA or UK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because RACEHIO keeps information on your device, most of these actions are available directly in the app. For anything else, write to soundshare.support@gmail.com.

14. Security

Information is kept in app storage protected by iOS. Car files sent to nearby devices are encrypted; the share request and nearby race data go directly between devices without encryption by RACEHIO. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.
