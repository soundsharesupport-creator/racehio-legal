2. What RACEHIO is

RACEHIO is a game in which you make cars and race them in practice or with people nearby. Nearby races and nearby sharing work directly between devices, without a RACEHIO account or server, and the devices do not need to be on the same Wi-Fi network. We cannot guarantee that nearby devices will always find each other or stay connected for a whole race.

3. RACEHIO Free

RACEHIO Free includes practice, nearby races, replays, and importing and sharing cars. You have one car of your own, which you can change at any time; if you delete it, you can make a new one. You can race with any car in your library: the sample cars and the cars friends send you. Cars you received from other people can be raced and shared, but not changed.

RACEHIO Garage is an optional auto-renewable yearly subscription sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase. With it you can make as many cars as you like, and change and duplicate your own cars and the sample cars. Cars you received from other people stay as their authors made them.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account. The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; you can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings. The price is shown in the app and in the App Store for your country. Restore Purchases makes an active subscription available again on your devices. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies and applicable law.

If the subscription ends, all your cars stay in your library and can still be raced and shared. You can then change one car of your own, as on RACEHIO Free; RACEHIO asks you which one.
