About RACEHIO

RACEHIO lets you make your own car — its body, paint, parts and wheels — and race it on the track: in practice against sample cars, or with friends nearby over the local network. There is no RACEHIO account and no central game server.

Your profile, cars, race history and replays are stored on this device. Your nickname, avatar and the car you race with are sent to nearby devices only during a nearby race or when you share a car; see the Privacy Policy.

Driving

  • Steer by tilting the phone left and right.
  • Gas and brake: hold the pedals on the screen. Hold the brake when stopped to reverse.
  • Camera: the camera button switches between the view behind the car and the view from the bonnet.

After the finish you can watch the replay of the race, and later again from Race History.

Free and Garage

  • RACEHIO Free: one car of your own, which you can change at any time (delete it to make a new one); race with any car, including sample cars and friends’ cars; import and share cars freely; practice and nearby races included.
  • RACEHIO Garage (yearly subscription): make as many cars as you like; change and duplicate your own cars and the sample cars.
  • Friends’ cars can be raced and shared, but only their authors can change them.
  • If Garage ends, all your cars stay. You can change one of your own cars, as on Free; RACEHIO asks you which one.

Prices shown in the app come from the App Store (StoreKit). If a price cannot be loaded, purchase stays disabled until StoreKit provides one.

Restore purchases

Open Plus on the Home screen and tap Restore Purchases. Sign in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. Restore recovers subscriptions Apple already links to that account; it does not create a new purchase.

Refunds

Refunds for App Store subscriptions are handled by Apple. Request a refund through Apple (for example reportaproblem.apple.com or Settings → [your name] → Media & Purchases → Purchase History). RACEHIO cannot issue App Store refunds directly.

Nearby races and Local Network

If devices cannot find each other, allow Local Network for RACEHIO on every device (Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network) and keep RACEHIO open on screen. Discovery is not guaranteed in every environment.

Hidden players

If someone nearby sends you invitations or cars you don’t want, tap ⋮ on their invitation card or on the incoming car and choose Hide player. You won’t get race invitations or cars from them any more. To see them again, open Settings › Hidden Players and tap Show. To report content or behaviour, write to the support address above.

Photo finish and videos

From the results and from Race History you can save or share the photo-finish picture, and save the replay of a race as a video. RACEHIO asks for permission to add to Photos only when you save; it never reads your library.
